# Requirements

## MVP
- Register, log in, log out and manage a profile.
- Discover, create, join and manage public/private communities.
- Publish, edit, remove and comment on posts.
- Create events and register/cancel attendance with capacity enforcement.
- Submit and moderate business listings.
- Publish opportunities and submit/review applications.
- Submit reports, review moderation queues and receive notifications.
- Responsive public and authenticated experiences.

Out of scope: real-time messaging, voice/video, payments, native mobile apps, complex AI and live streaming.
